What you get

One visit, with the same detail-first checklist as every other job we do.

  • A one-time clean of the home before you move in or after you move out.
  • Add-ons, picked in the quote form and included in your estimate:

    • Interior oven

  • Our own cleaning supplies, brought to every clean.
  • Licensed and insured on every job.

Who it's for

  • Renters moving out of a home in Lake Havasu City
  • Anyone moving in who wants the place cleaned first

How it works

  1. Get an instant estimate
