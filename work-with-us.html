Clean with Spotless Cleaning in Lake Havasu City

We’re hiring reliable, detail-minded cleaners as hourly employees. No experience needed: we train you. If that sounds like you, the application takes about two minutes.

At a glance
  • Hourly employee (W-2)
  • Lake Havasu City only
  • Homes, rentals and offices
  • $18/hour while training, then $20/hour

The work

  • Residential cleaning: standard and deep cleans for homes, one-time and recurring.
  • Vacation rental and Airbnb turnovers between checkout and check-in.
  • Move-in and move-out cleaning.
  • Commercial and office cleaning, often after hours.
  • Part-time, flexible hours. Rental turnovers usually run 10 AM to 4 PM, and weekends are busiest in season.
  • We’ll cover supplies and equipment at your interview.

What we look for

  • You show up when you said you would, and text if something changes.
  • You notice the small stuff: baseboards, fixtures, the spot behind the toilet.
  • Reliable transportation to jobs around Lake Havasu City.
  • An open schedule, including some weekends.
  • Experience is a plus, not a requirement. Your first month is training alongside an experienced cleaner.

Pay

Pay: $18 an hour for your first month while you train, then $20 an hour, with raises after that.

How and when you’re paid: We’ll go over the pay schedule at your interview.

This is a regular hourly job (W-2 employee), so taxes are withheld from your paycheck like any other job.
