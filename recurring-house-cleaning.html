Frequently asked questions

How much do recurring plans save?

Compared with a one-time clean, monthly visits save 5%, every-2-weeks visits save 10% and weekly visits save 15% per visit.

Do you offer weekly and biweekly cleaning?

Yes. We offer weekly and every-2-weeks cleaning, and monthly too. The more often we visit, the more you save per visit.

Can I add extras to a visit?

Yes. Pets in the home, interior windows, dishes, interior fridge, interior oven and laundry (per load) are add-ons. Select them in the quote form and they are included in your estimate.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes. We bring our own cleaning supplies.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Spotless Cleaning is licensed and insured, and locally owned and operated in Lake Havasu City, AZ.
