Frequently asked questions

How does the price estimate work?

Enter the type of cleaning, square footage, bedrooms, bathrooms, frequency and any add-ons in the quote form and you see your estimate when you submit. It is a starting estimate; we confirm the final number after a quick follow-up, and it may vary once we see the space.

Do recurring plans cost less?

Yes. Compared with a one-time clean, monthly visits save 5%, every-2-weeks visits save 10% and weekly visits save 15% per visit.

What types of cleaning do you offer?

Residential standard and deep cleaning, move-in/move-out cleaning, commercial cleaning, and vacation rental / Airbnb turnovers.

What add-ons are available?

Pets in the home, interior windows, dishes, interior fridge, interior oven and laundry (per load). Select them in the quote form and they are included in your estimate.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Spotless Cleaning is licensed and insured, and locally owned and operated in Lake Havasu City, AZ.

Where do you work?

Residential and commercial properties across the Lake Havasu City, AZ area.

How do I book or ask a question?
