Vacation rental turnover checklist

PropertyDateCleaned by

1. Arrival walk-through

  • Photos of damage, stains, missing items (before cleaning)
  • Left-behind items collected in one place
  • Repairs noted for the host
  • Windows opened or fans on to air out

2. Start laundry and trash

  • Beds stripped, first load started
  • Towels and bath mats collected
  • Trash and recycling out from every room
  • Dishes loaded or washed

3. Kitchen

  • Guest food out of fridge; shelves and handles wiped
  • Microwave, stovetop, oven door
  • Coffee maker emptied and wiped
  • Counters, sink, appliance fronts
  • Dishes put away, full sets in cabinets
  • New trash and recycling liners

4. Bathrooms

  • Toilet, shower, tub, sink, mirror
  • Hair off drains, floor, shower walls
  • Water spots off faucets and glass
  • Fresh towels and mats, folded the same way
  • Trash emptied, new liner

5. Bedrooms

  • Fresh linens, beds made the same way
  • Mattress protectors and pillows checked
  • Nightstands, lamps, headboards dusted
  • Under beds, drawers and closets checked

6. Living areas

  • Fans and fixtures dusted, top down
  • Cushions straightened and checked under
  • Remotes wiped and in their spot
  • Switches, handles, slider glass

7. Outdoor and lake gear

  • Sand swept from entries, patio, garage
  • Patio furniture wiped; sunscreen marks checked
  • Grill grates and grease tray
  • Coolers and lake gear rinsed, dried, put back
  • Pool and beach towels to laundry

8. Restock

  • Toilet paper, paper towels, tissues
  • Hand soap, dish soap, dishwasher detergent
  • Shampoo, conditioner, body wash
  • Trash bags, coffee, kitchen basics
  • Low supplies noted for the host

9. Floors and final check

  • Vacuumed and mopped, ending at the door
  • Rooms match the listing photos
  • Thermostat set as the host wants
  • Lights off, windows locked, blinds set
  • Door code or lockbox reset
  • Photo of the finished place sent to host
