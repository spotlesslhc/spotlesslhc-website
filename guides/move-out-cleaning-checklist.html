Move-out cleaning checklist for Lake Havasu renters

A room-by-room checklist for handing back a rental, in the order that keeps you from cleaning anything twice. Tick items off on your phone as you go.

When you move out of a rental, cleaning is the part of the handover you control the most. Landlords and property managers usually compare the place with how it looked when you moved in, so the goal is simple: hand it back looking the way you got it.

The rule that saves the most time is top to bottom, dry before wet. Dust falls, so clean high surfaces before counters, and counters before floors. Vacuum or sweep before you mop, or you end up pushing grit around.

Before you start

Kitchen

The kitchen takes the longest, so start here while you have energy. Get the oven going first if it needs a soak or a self-clean cycle.

Bathrooms

Hard water. Water in Lake Havasu City tends to be hard, so faucets, shower glass and showerheads collect white mineral spots. A vinegar soak or a cleaner made for mineral deposits works better than scrubbing harder. Test it on a small spot first, and keep acidic cleaners off natural stone.

Done in order, most apartments and smaller homes take a full day. A larger house with a garage can take more than that, so plan for it before your lease ends rather than the night before.
