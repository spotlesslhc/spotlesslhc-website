While you're here

Put cleaning on a standing plan for the months you're in town and stop thinking about it. The more consistent the schedule, the more you save per visit.

How a season works

  1. Before you arrive

    Get an instant estimate in the quote form, or call (928) 228-5899, daily 9 AM to 9 PM. We confirm the details and have the home clean for your arrival.

  2. While you're here

    Pick weekly, every 2 weeks or monthly in the quote form to see the savings applied. Your standing visit goes on the calendar for your stay.

  3. When you head home

    A close-up clean when you leave, so the home is clean for the summer or for the next renter. PLACEHOLDER (Bryce): anything a close-up clean adds, like emptying the fridge or taking out the trash, or say it's the same checklist.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Can you clean my home before I arrive for the winter?

Yes. A pre-arrival clean means the home is ready when you walk in after a long drive.

Can I have a standing plan just for the months I'm here?

Yes. Pick weekly, every 2 weeks or monthly for your stay. Compared with a one-time clean, monthly visits save 5%, every-2-weeks visits save 10% and weekly visits save 15% per visit.

Do you clean when I leave in the spring?

Yes. A close-up clean when you head home leaves the place clean for the summer or for the next renter.

Do you watch the house or hold keys while I'm away?

No. We clean; we don't offer home watch or key holding.

Do you clean furnished winter rentals for property managers?

Yes. We clean furnished seasonal rentals before guests arrive and after they leave. Call (928) 228-5899 to set it up.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Spotless Cleaning is licensed and insured, and locally owned and operated in Lake Havasu City, AZ.
